# Privacy Policy – EchoMind Voice Notes

EchoMind respects your privacy.

## Information We Collect
EchoMind processes voice recordings and transcripts to generate summaries, key points, and action items. Audio recordings may be temporarily transmitted to AI services for transcription and analysis.

## How We Use Information
The information is used solely to provide AI-powered note generation and productivity features.

## Data Storage
Voice recordings and generated notes are stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

## Third-Party Services
EchoMind may use third-party services, including AI processing providers and subscription services, to operate the app.

## Subscriptions
EchoMind offers optional subscriptions that unlock additional features such as unlimited recordings and advanced export options.
